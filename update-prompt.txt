### Group 1: FX & Rates

**Rate Fragility Index (RFI)**
Do not search externally — calculate from the formula embedded in the HTML comment block
above the <!-- MARKET DATA TAB --> section. Formula:
  RFI = -0.6816 + (0.0549 × JGB_10Y) + (0.1852 × UST_30Y)
Bear-steepener bonus: add +0.04 to +0.06 when (UST_30Y > 5.0%) AND (UST_30Y − UST_10Y > 1.0%).
Use the JGB 10Y and UST 30Y values you find below to compute. Round to 2 decimal places.
